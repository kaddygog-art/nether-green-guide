A guest's field guide

Welcome to Nether Green

Sheffield · S11 7EJ

You're staying in a quiet, leafy corner of south-west Sheffield. The woods of the Porter Valley are minutes away on foot, the city centre is 15 minutes by car, and the moors of the Peak District start about 20 minutes up the road. Here's where to eat, walk and explore.

Arriving: self check-in

  1. Find the house. From the road, walk up the gravel path between the two tall hedges. There’s free parking on the road outside.
  2. Go to your door. Yours is No. 6.
  3. Get the keys. The keys are in a key safe beside the door. We'll send you the code in your Airbnb messages on the day you arrive.
  4. Settle in. Connect to the Wi-Fi, set the heating to suit you and make yourself at home.
  5. Let us know you're in. A quick message on Airbnb helps us know you arrived safely.

Worth the trip

Kelham Island, just north of the city centre, is Sheffield's old steelmaking quarter. These days it's where the city goes to eat and drink. It's about 20 minutes by taxi, or take the 120 into town and walk 15 minutes.
